Sector-wise inflation

The new CPI classifies spending into twelve COICOP divisions. Bars show the latest year-on-year rate for each division; the heatmap tracks how each rate has evolved month by month under the new series, coloured by distance from the 4% target.

What is driving headline inflation

Each division’s pull on the headline rate is its inflation weighted by its share of the consumption basket. Food & beverages carries 36.8% of the basket, so even modest food inflation moves the total. Contributions are derived from published division indices, published year-on-year rates, and the official weighting diagram.

Data & method notes

  • Source. All figures are fetched at render time from the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementations as reported on the eSankhyiki portal.
  • Base switch. MoSPI moved the CPI to base 2024 = 100 (COICOP 2018, 12 divisions) with the January 2026 release. This page uses the 2012-base series through December 2025 and the 2024-base series from January 2026; year-on-year rates are as published for each series, so the chained trend matches official releases.
  • Weights are the official weighting diagram from MoSPI’s first 2024-base press release (12 Feb 2026): Food & beverages 36.75, Housing & utilities 17.67, Transport 8.80, Clothing 6.38, Health 6.10, Personal care & misc 5.04, Household goods 4.47, Info & communication 3.61, Restaurants 3.35, Education 3.33, Paan & tobacco 2.99, Recreation 1.52 (All-India Combined).
  • Contributions are computed as \(w_g\,(I_{g,t}-I_{g,t-12}) \,/\, \sum_h w_h I_{h,t-12}\), where last year’s index is recovered from the published year-on-year rate. They reproduce the headline rate up to rounding in the published indices.
  • State map. State/UT rates are the CPI (General) year-on-year change for the Combined sector, from the same API. Boundaries are as published by the Government of India (Survey of India depiction — Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh shown in full), taken from the district GeoJSON of udit-001/india-maps-data and dissolved to state level.
